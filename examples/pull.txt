HOOK TAROT  three-card pull
Topic: my spring retreat
Audience: burnt-out founders

1. WHAT THEY BELIEVE (past)  Six of Wands: The Victory Lap
   The belief your reader walks in with.
   Structure:    I {achieved result} without {the thing everyone thinks is required}.
   Example:      I sold out my autumn retreat without a single paid ad.
   Pull it when: A true result that breaks a myth. Only if you can show it.

2. WHAT YOU'LL SAY (present)  The Golden Hour (XIX, Major Arcana)
   The line that turns it.
   Structure:    The best moment of {experience} isn't {the expected moment}. It's {one small true moment}.
   Example:      The best moment of a retreat isn't the closing ceremony. It's day two, when someone laughs for the first time.
   Pull it when: Sell the feeling by pointing at one small, true moment.

3. WHAT THEY'LL DO (future)  Ace of Swords: The Sharp Question
   The move they make after reading.
   Structure:    What would {outcome} look like if {constraint} didn't exist?
   Example:      What would your business look like if you never had to post again?
   Pull it when: Open a door in her head with one clean question.
